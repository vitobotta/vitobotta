## ⭐ hetzner-k3s — production Kubernetes on Hetzner Cloud, in minutes

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vitobotta/hetzner-k3s/main/logo-v2.png" width="90" align="right" alt="hetzner-k3s logo">

A single CLI, written in Crystal, that creates a fully configured and highly available k3s cluster on Hetzner Cloud. No Terraform. No management cluster. One binary and a YAML file.

- **3,700+ stars · 235 forks · tested at 500+ nodes**
- 3-master / 3-worker HA cluster in 2–3 minutes
- Autoscaling, private networking, firewalls and encrypted storage handled for you
- Up to ~80% cheaper than AWS, GCP or Azure for the same workload

[Documentation](https://hetzner-k3s.com) · [Source](https://github.com/vitobotta/hetzner-k3s) · [Discussions](https://github.com/vitobotta/hetzner-k3s/discussions) · ❤️ [Sponsor it](https://github.com/sponsors/vitobotta)

## 🎬 StreamVault — your own streaming service

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vitobotta/streamvault/main/public/icon.png" width="90" align="right" alt="StreamVault icon">

Self-hosted Rails app for discovering, organising and watching your own media in the browser. Connect your RealDebrid account and it picks the lightest playback path the browser can handle: direct play, remux, or FFmpeg transcode.

![StreamVault](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vitobotta/streamvault/main/docs/screenshots/home.jpg)

- Multiple audio tracks, embedded and external subtitles, 17 languages
- Personal library, wishlist, continue watching and progress sync
- Docker or native macOS, with VideoToolbox acceleration on Apple Silicon

[streamvault.page](https://streamvault.page) · [Source](https://github.com/vitobotta/streamvault)

## 🧪 SprintPulse — retrospective tooling for teams (a side business)

<img src="https://sprintpulse.io/icon.png" width="90" align="right" alt="SprintPulse">

Not open source, but worth a look if you run agile teams. SprintPulse is the retrospective tool I founded and run: structured retros, AI-assisted insight extraction, and a history of actions your team actually follows through on — instead of a Miro board nobody opens again.

- Async-friendly retros for distributed teams
- Turns scattered sticky notes into themes, decisions and owned actions
- Integrations with the tools your team already uses

👉 **[sprintpulse.io](https://sprintpulse.io/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=profile&utm_content=profile)** — free trial, no credit card.
